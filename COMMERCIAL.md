## What the Guide Covers

**8 parts. ~90 minutes end-to-end.**

- **Part 1 — The First Decision**: Why treating Secretary as a chatbot is the mistake everyone makes in session one. What "chief of staff mode" actually looks like.

- **Part 2 — Your Intent File**: The most underused file in the repo. How to write it, why skipping it costs 30 minutes of re-briefing per week, and the three fields that actually change behavior.

- **Part 3 — Designing Your First Worker**: The two decisions that determine everything downstream — what a Worker owns, and what it decides autonomously vs. escalates. Includes the minimal role file that actually works.

- **Part 4 — The Dispatch System**: What a good dispatch looks like vs. a bad one. The six mistakes that cause Workers to produce adjacent results instead of correct ones. The `done_when` field is the most important part — here's why.

- **Part 5 — Context Management**: The hard part. Why context compression happens, what the wrong state-write order costs you, and the three-trigger rule that prevents context loss.

- **Part 6 — When to Add AR Manager**: The sign you're ready. What AR Manager owns vs. what Secretary owns. When NOT to add it.

- **Part 7 — Ensemble Sessions**: When multiple Workers give you better decisions than one. How to structure one, and how to read the output.

- **Part 8 — Mistakes I Made**: Seven specific mistakes from the first four weeks — what happened and how to avoid them.
